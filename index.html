WILDERNESS
SURVIVAL

You wake up in a deep forest with empty pockets and no idea how you got here. Chop, gather, fish, farm and build. Eat before you starve, stay warm after dark, and watch the treeline at night: wolves hunt when the light dies.

This is real survival. Hunger, cold and teeth can all end the run.

THE FOREST WINS

FIELD NOTES

Food: berries from bushes, fish from the pond, potatoes from crops you plant. Raw meat and fish can turn your stomach. Cook at a campfire.
Warmth: nights are cold enough to kill. Stand near a lit fire, build a tent, or finish the cabin. Rain makes everything colder.
Fires: burn through fuel. Face the fire and press Act to cook food first, or feed it wood when your pack has no raw food.
Fishing: face the pond with a rod, press Act to cast, then press Act again the instant you see the splash.
Wolves: come after 9 PM and will not cross firelight. An axe ends an argument in two swings.
Sleep: at a tent, cabin, or lit fire. Sleeping grows crops, regrows berries, and costs you a night's hunger.
Goal: survive, then thrive. A cabin by the pond means you made it. Keys: WASD move · E act · F eat · C craft · G goals
